If this existed before, I probably would not have gone down the path of building my own from scratch.  A monoblock that can plop down on a laptop is goated, split is nice at a desk, but nothing beats the portability of a monoblock.  I don't like how this one covers up the trackpad with its 4th set of thumb keys.  Also there's no need to make the sides so thin, the height in the middle goes up to the top of the caps. Doing this all the way around makes it so much easier to toss into a backpack without touching any keys.  almost there dygma, just a few nitpicks.
