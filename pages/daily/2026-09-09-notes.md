## plaindown

Major ui rewrite on plaindown mobile today.  Yesterday I wrapped up a huge  change for perf, its now typing absolutely clean and rendering fast!  Every keystroke is coming through with no stutter.  I mean zero stutter.  It might be a smidge slow to start up, but once it's going nothing is seemign to slow it down today.

I did 4 sol sessions today, that might be a stretch as 2 ran out of tokens, it was 2 full five hour limits on a mobile ui rewrite to get it really usable.  Publish fires it off, no need for unnecessary review to create a file.  Clean ui that does not need to scroll sideways to discover the save.
