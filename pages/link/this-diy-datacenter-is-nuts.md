I have so much more appreciation for Hetzner after seeing how the sausage is made.  Their drive for efficiency is unparalleled.  Not top tier performance, straight up well done engineering on the things that matter.  No showing off or fancy stuff.  The way they rack up their machines on a piece of sheet metal, that Linus likened to a desktop PC case without the front and side panels was a chef's kiss.
