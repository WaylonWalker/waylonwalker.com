We are entering into dark times, dark times I tell ya.  An era where we have all the greatest tools to build software by hand, with decades of experience to do it.  Yet it's a time where none of it matters.  Machines can spit out glorious amounts of slop in an instant.  They can do weeks of the average mans work in minutes.  Yet the core principles still apply now more than ever.  Observability into our production systems matter more than ever.  Things are changing fast, and big.  Prs are not the same size they used to be.  We have not yet developed the tools and intuition into this new world to manage it effectively.  This will come with time, but for now, these are dark times filled with the glitter and glisten of promise, hope, and shiny new things cranked out upon first thought
