This question came up in Syntax.fm show today, someone was very hesitant to adopt agents, claimed they had spent a lot of this year thinking about a project and was not sure which agent to try.

## Are you cooked

Heck no, in fact maybe you are better off than many of us with psycosis.  Those still writing code by hand with good reason, hats off to you.

## As an interviewer am I concerned

100% _(but it totally depends on the job you are trying to get)_.  I felt a lot of concern with the way the question was worded.  The caller claimed to have spent a lot of time thinking about trying a project.  Knew of many agents to try, but was afraid to do anything.

What are you trying to do?  Are you in it for the love of crafting code from your brain, for building a product, do you want a job? where?  At some point you gotta see the job market is moving on, its looking for people with skills in wrangling agents because this has become the most effetive way to get shit done.  The world will always need some people who can do it by hand.  The path you choose is your choice, where the market takes your work is not.
