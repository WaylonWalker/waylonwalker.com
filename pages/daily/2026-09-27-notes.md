### plaindown

Over the past few months I've hit a rythm building out my own text editor that pairs up with markata.  It's been great to have something that is super customized to my workflow, to markata-go, building out features that no other editor would have.  What has been more inspiring is to see Rhiannon actually start using it.  Somehow I've landed with something that not only works for me, but works for her too.  She has been writing away on her own site, and I've recently redone her site to really theme it into what she likes and its looking **really** good.

### work

I'm really proud of being able to see issues that we were having around finding, previewing, getting users to upload valid input data and putting something together to meet users where they are in a way that feels like what they were using but polished to a high level and working really good.
