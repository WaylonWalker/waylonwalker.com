I started this site as a place to keep my own work.  Since 2017, it has
moved between hosts, changed generators, and grown from a blog into a home
for notes, thoughts, and pings.  The domain and the writing came along for
the ride.

This history runs from oldest to newest.  It draws on repository records and
my own recollections.  Dates identify the records or events described, not
necessarily the day a service went live or shut down.

## 2017–2020 · A place of my own

The first version gave me a domain and a place to publish after a rough year
in tech.  The tools and hosting started changing almost immediately.

:::: road editorial story

### :lucide-globe: A domain and a blog

*May 2017*

I registered the domain and started making content in 2017.  The older
[`waylonwalker.github.io`](https://github.com/WaylonWalker/waylonwalker.github.io)
repository has a `CNAME` for both site addresses, GitHub Pages enabled, and
Pelican configuration alongside a blog.  It looks like the first version was
powered by Pelican on GitHub Pages.

### :lucide-code: A Gatsby rebuild

*January 2019*

The [`waylonwalkerv2`](https://github.com/WaylonWalker/waylonwalkerv2)
repository is a Gatsby 2 site with waylonwalker.com as its homepage.  It is
now labeled deprecated, though it kept receiving commits until 2023.

Archived spring 2019 experiments include
[`gatsby-starter-blog-forestry`](https://github.com/WaylonWalker/gatsby-starter-blog-forestry),
[`TestGatsbySanity`](https://github.com/WaylonWalker/TestGatsbySanity), and
[Netlify and Fauna demos](https://github.com/WaylonWalker/netlify-faunadb-example).
They look like CMS and serverless trials around the main site, rather than
separate versions of the domain.

### :lucide-cloud: A new host

*By February 2020*

I wrote that the site had been on Netlify for nearly two years.  That puts the
move from GitHub Pages around 2018, while the Gatsby rebuild was taking
shape.  [The deployment writeup](/should-i-switch-to-zeit-now/) gives me a
window, not an exact cutover date.

## 2021–2023 · Making the tools

The site became a place to build the publishing machinery too.  Markdown and
frontmatter stayed with me even as the generator and host changed.

:::: road editorial story

### :lucide-hammer: Markata takes shape

*March–June 2021*

In March I started a new site built with Markata, the static site generator I
was making for myself.  The [first commit](https://github.com/waylonwalker/waylonwalker.com/commit/f3aad49a)
is literally called `init v3`.  I started collecting
[notes](https://github.com/waylonwalker/waylonwalker.com/commit/9da75145)
in the site repo that spring.

### :lucide-cloud: Another move

*By October 2021*

My [deployment writeup](/colophon/) says I had moved from Netlify to
Cloudflare Pages.  The writing could stay in the repository while its home
on the internet changed again.

### :lucide-message-circle: Thoughts join the feed

*July–September 2023*

The separate [thoughts project](https://git.waylonwalker.com/waylon/thoughts/commit/b922026)
started in July with a Fly.io configuration.  In September I added the
[thoughts plugin](https://github.com/waylonwalker/waylonwalker.com/commit/c5c28bad)
to pull short posts into the site alongside longer articles.

### :lucide-server: The homelab joins in

*October–December 2023*

The [screenshot service](https://git.wayl.one/waylon/shot-scraper-api/commit/3fa493c)
was using Kubernetes by October.  I started running
[Kubernetes at home](/kubernetes-6-months-in/) in December.  The main site
still had another move ahead of it.

## 2024–2026 · A basement home

More of the site's supporting tools moved into the homelab.  The generator
changed again, but the collection of writing kept growing.

:::: road editorial story

### :lucide-hard-drive: Media gets a home

*December 2024*

[Dropper](https://git.wayl.one/waylon/dropper/commit/a11cd70) began storing
my media.  It became another piece of the workshop around the site.

### :lucide-house: The site moves home

*January–September 2025*

The homelab repo added a [build job](https://github.com/WaylonWalker/homelab-argo/commit/b5acd1d)
for the main site in January.  I tried [Forgejo](/trying-forgejo/) in August,
and by September I was [building the site in Kubernetes](/why-make-a-website-in-2025/).
Cloudflare Tunnel now carries the self-hosted site out to the internet.

### :lucide-message-square: Pings arrive

*December 2025*

[Pings](https://github.com/waylonwalker/waylonwalker.com/commit/eef61427)
gave me another way to publish smaller bits while the idea is still warm.
Notes, thoughts, and pings scratch a similar itch: get the thing out of my
head and somewhere I can find it again.

### :lucide-refresh-cw: Markata moves to Go

*January–April 2026*

I began moving the site from Python Markata to Markata Go in
[January](https://github.com/waylonwalker/waylonwalker.com/commit/be02be8c).
A [new home page](https://github.com/waylonwalker/waylonwalker.com/commit/c7d6f82d)
took shape around the growing pile of posts and small updates.  The
[migration branch landed](https://github.com/waylonwalker/waylonwalker.com/commit/a26fc215)
in April, another chance to shape the machinery around how I actually write.

### :lucide-box: Thoughts joins the cluster

*May 2026*

The homelab Argo repo added a
[Kubernetes application](https://github.com/WaylonWalker/homelab-argo/commit/dd37bf7)
for thoughts.  The service had stayed on Fly.io for years while the main site
pulled its posts into the feed.

## Reading the record

This timeline combines commit history and repository metadata from the old
GitHub site repositories, the Forgejo-hosted thoughts, Dropper, and
shot-scraper-api projects, and the homelab and homelab-argo repositories.
I cross-checked those with recollections in [about this site](/about-this-site/),
the deployment writeups, and the [colophon](/colophon/).  The 2017 starting
point has both my recollection and the first domain repo behind it.  Hosting
cutovers are less completely recorded than code changes.
